How it works

Three steps.
Under five minutes.

From the moment a coordinator hits publish to the moment a substitute is confirmed, the loop is short on purpose. Care can't wait for spreadsheets.

01

Coordinator posts the shift

Three-step form. Pick the role and time, set urgency, write a one-line note. Hit publish. The draft autosaves so you can finish on your phone.

30s avg time to publish
02

Staffig matches the right people

The eligibility engine ranks every active substitute by distance, language, qualifications, certifications, and availability. Top 12 get an urgent push. The rest are notified on a tiered fallback.

96% median match accuracy
03

Substitute accepts in one tap

From the lock screen — no app open required. The service worker calls accept, the coordinator's workflow card lights up live, and the substitute sees a confirmed shift on their schedule.

54s median response (urgent)

Getting started

Set up once.
Get the right shifts.

Invited substitutes go through a 3-step onboarding: qualifications, certifications, availability. The eligibility engine takes it from there — no inbox spam for shifts you can't take.

Tell us once
what you can do.

Pick your role, qualifications, certifications, and languages. Add the neighbourhoods you'll travel to. Set your weekly availability on a 7×4 grid.

Qualifications — AEC, DEC TEE, DEC PSW, RN diploma…
Certifications — CPR, first aid, police check, vulnerable sector. Upload docs once.
Availability — recurring weekly slots or one-off days, urgent toggle for sick-call alerts.
Distance — set a max radius. We won't notify you for shifts beyond it.
